# Attribution and reuse

**Project author:** Jackson Josephat Kavishe. Developed while learning Power BI through **edX**, then updated with economic and population observations through 2025.

The exact course/provider title was not supplied with the publication materials, so no more specific course credit or claim of wholly independent design is made. Course-provided instructions, templates or icons retain their original terms.

**Economic, demographic and geographic data:** World Bank, World Development Indicators, with the underlying sources identified on its indicator pages. See [GDP](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.KD), [population](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL) and [land area](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/AG.LND.TOTL.K2). These indicator pages identify CC BY 4.0; retain the provider and underlying source attribution when reusing the data.

**Temperature data:** NASA Goddard Institute for Space Studies, GISTEMP v4, global annual land-ocean temperature anomalies relative to 1951–1980. Source: [NASA GISTEMP](https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/). NASA's [download page](https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/data_v4.html) provides source data and citation guidance.

**Screenshots:** supplied by the project author and published to demonstrate report functionality.

This repository makes the report and supporting material available for portfolio review. It does not add a blanket license over course assets, provider data or third-party icons. Each component retains its applicable terms.
